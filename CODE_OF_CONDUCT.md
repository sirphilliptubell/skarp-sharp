# Code of Conduct

The behavior expected from everyone who takes part in the Skarp project.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors are new to the topic or write in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on code, not on people. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project.
- Keep discussion on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexual attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Spam, or repeated pressure on the maintainer to merge or reply.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to behavior outside them when it affects people in the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, and block people from the repository. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban.

## Reporting

To report a problem, send a private message to the maintainer on GitHub, or open a private security advisory on the repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately. Please do not post them in public threads.
